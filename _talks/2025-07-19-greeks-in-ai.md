I attended the inaugural [Greeks in AI 2025](https://www.greeksin.ai/) symposium (19–20 July 2025) at the Serafeio of the City of Athens. The event brought together the Greek AI community around research presentations, keynotes, and discussions spanning machine learning, computer vision, and related areas.
