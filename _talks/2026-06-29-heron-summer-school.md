I attended the [1st Summer School in Robotics and AI](https://heron-robotics-coe.eu/1st-summer-school-in-robotics-and-ai/) (29–30 June 2026), organized by HERON – Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence through the Robotics Institute of Athena Research Center, in collaboration with the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT).

The two-day program covered recent topics at the intersection of robotics and AI, including robot perception, multimodal AI, humanoid robotics, and large language and vision-language models in robotics.
